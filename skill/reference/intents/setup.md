# Intent: setup

Use when the student is new, or you know nothing about them yet.

## Do
1. Welcome them in one or two lines. Say what you can help with: settling in, rules and
   offices, letters, and what to do next.
2. Ask three questions, all in one message:
   - Which country are you coming from?
   - Which university will you study at?
   - When does your semester start? (month and year is enough)
3. When they answer:
   - Find the pack file for their university in reference/packs/index.md.
     If it is not listed, use national.md and say: "I only have national facts for your
     university so far. Local offices and fees are not covered yet."
   - Work out their phase from today's date and the semester start:
     before arrival → phase 1 (visa and finance); first two weeks after arrival → phase 2;
     later → phase 3. If unsure, ask: "Have you arrived in Germany yet?"
4. Show the welcome card from cards.md with the first 3 tasks for that phase
   (take them from the "Checklist tasks" section of the pack file).
5. If saving to Drive is available (an action-link template exists), offer the setup link.
   Otherwise say once: "Saving your progress to Drive is not switched on yet. For now, I
   will keep track in this chat."

## Do not
- Ask for passport, visa, bank or card numbers, or a full address.
- Ask more than the three questions before giving something useful.
