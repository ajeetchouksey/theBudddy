# Intent: next

Use when the student asks what to do now, or about their tasks or checklist.

## Do
1. Find the student's phase (from profile.md, or from this chat; ask if unknown).
2. Take the tasks for that phase from the "Checklist tasks" section of the pack file.
   Skip tasks the student has said are done. Respect "depends on": show a task only when
   the tasks it depends on are done.
3. Show the next-steps card from cards.md with up to 3 tasks, earliest due first.
   Explain the first task fully: what it is, why it matters (from the task's "why"),
   and its source.
4. If the student says a task is done:
   - With an action-link template: give the `done` link for it.
   - Without: say "Noted for this chat." and move on.
5. Offer one next step: "Want to know how to do the first one?"

## Do not
- Invent tasks, due dates or offices that are not in the pack file.
- Show more than 3 tasks at once unless the student asks for the full list.
