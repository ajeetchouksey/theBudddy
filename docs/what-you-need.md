# What You Need — Germany Student Companion

A plain guide to the accounts and apps you need to use the companion. Nothing to buy.

## What you need

Three things, all free:

1. **A personal Google account** (Gmail). Your checklist, budget and notes are saved here, and only you own them.
2. **One AI app:** Gemini, Claude or Microsoft Copilot. The free version is enough.
3. **A phone or laptop with a web browser.** Both work. You can use both.

You don't need to install anything else, pay for anything, or know how to code.

## Choose your AI app

Use the app you already like. The companion works the same way in all three, and you can switch later without losing anything.

|  | Gemini | Claude | Microsoft Copilot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Works on the free plan | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Reads your Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar | Yes, built in | Yes, after you connect Google | Yes, after you connect Google |
| Good if you… | Already use Google apps a lot | Like long, careful explanations | Use Windows or Outlook too |

**Free or paid?** Free is enough for everything important: your checklist, letters, budget, reminders and questions. A paid plan may add extras such as charts or dashboards. We will never ask you to upgrade.

**Personal or university Google account?** Use your **personal** account. University accounts sometimes have fewer features, and you lose them when you graduate.

*We are still testing each app. This table will be updated after testing in October 2026.*

## Connect your Google account

The companion asks you to do this in your first chat, if it is needed. It takes about a minute.

- **Gemini:** nothing to do if you are signed in with your Google account. Make sure Google apps (Drive, Gmail, Calendar) are switched on in Gemini's settings.
- **Claude:** open Claude's connectors and switch on Google Drive, Gmail and Google Calendar. Sign in with your Google account when asked.
- **Copilot:** open your profile, then Connectors, and connect Google Drive, Gmail and Google Calendar. On copilot.com you can also turn connectors on with the + button in a chat.

**Not able to connect?** You can still use the companion. Paste or upload letters into the chat, and tap the "My status" link when it asks you to.

## What gets created in your Drive

When you tap "Set up my Drive", one private folder called **Germany Journey** appears in your Google Drive. Google may show a screen asking you to allow access; it only lets the companion's script use files it creates.

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Checklist | Your tasks, phase by phase, with due dates |
| Budget | Your spending, and how long your money will last |
| Log | Every change made through the companion, so you can undo it |
| `index.md` | Settings for the companion. Don't share it. |
| `profile.md` | Your university, course, start date and goals. No passport or bank numbers. |
| Letters, Documents, Courses | Folders for you to keep things tidy |
| Packs | A shortcut to the companion's checked facts |

You can open, read and edit all of it yourself. Delete the folder and everything is gone.

## Your privacy

Your information stays in your own Google account. The people who make the companion never receive it.

- **Your AI app** can see what you share in the chat, and what its Google connection lets it read. That is between you and the app you chose.
- **The companion's script** runs inside your Google account and can only touch the Germany Journey files it created.
- **We** only publish general facts about Germany. We don't run a server that stores anything about you.

**Never type these into any chat:** passport, visa, tax ID, bank account or card numbers. The companion will never ask for them.

**To turn things off:**

- Disconnect Google in your AI app's connector settings.
- Remove the script's access in your Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com), in the list of third-party apps and services connected to your account.
- Delete the Germany Journey folder to remove all your data.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Google says the app "isn't verified" | This can happen during the pilot. Ask us in the pilot group before you continue. |
| Tapping a link says "You need access" | Sign in to the same Google account you used for setup, then tap again. |
| A link says "already done" | That change was already saved. Nothing happened twice. |
| I made a mistake | Tap **Undo** on the result page, or tell the companion "undo my last change". |
| The companion can't see my Drive | Check the Google connection in your AI app. Or tap "My status" and paste the result. |
| I want to use a different AI app | Add the companion there and connect Google. It finds your Germany Journey folder by itself. |
| An answer looks wrong or out of date | Tell the companion "this looks wrong" and send us the source. We fix facts within 48 hours during the pilot. |
| I'm stressed or in trouble | Tell the companion. It will point you to people who can help. In an emergency, call 112. |
